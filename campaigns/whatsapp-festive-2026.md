## 1. The four facts that decide product selection

**Fact 1 — Ganesh revenue is about to fall off a cliff.**
Last 30 days, Ganesh SKUs did ~₹1.60L (Divine Ganesha ₹66k/49 orders, Throne Ganesha
₹54k/38, Trishul Ganesh ₹40k/27). Ganesh Chaturthi ended with visarjan on
**25 Sep 2026**. These SKUs have no occasion behind them until next year.

The SKUs that carry into Navratri → Dhanteras → Diwali are the **Lakshmi / Kuber /
Kamdhenu / Vastu** block:

Kuber Kalash is the **single best conversion product in the catalogue**: highest order
count, lowest price (₹1,249), pure wealth symbolism. It is the entry weapon for cold
cohorts. Lakshmi Charan and Shubh Labh show ~₹9.8k and ~₹3.5k per order — those are
**bulk/gifting buyers**, not single-unit shoppers.

**Fact 2 — There is a 14-day selling wall starting 27 Sep.**

| Date | Event | What it means for you |
|---|---|---|
| 25 Sep 2026 | Anant Chaturdashi / Ganesh Visarjan | Ganesh season closes |
| **26 Sep** | **Last clean selling day** | **Hard-sell window ends here** |
| 27 Sep – 10 Oct | **Pitru Paksha (Shraddh)** | Indian homes do not buy new murtis. Do NOT hard-sell. |
| 11 Oct | Navratri / Ghatasthapana | Demand switches back on |
| 20 Oct | Dussehra | Gifting spike |
| **6 Nov** | **Dhanteras** | **The single biggest silver-buying day of the year** |
| 8 Nov | Diwali | Peak |

This gives you genuine, culturally true urgency right now — "before Shraddh begins" is
not a fake countdown. Use it hard for 3 days, then go quiet on selling.

**Fact 3 — You are a low-discount brand. Do not break that.**
Discounts are running at only **3.3% of gross sales** (₹20,099 on ₹6.03L last peak week).
A blanket 20% WhatsApp offer is **6x your normal discount rate** and would destroy more
margin than it creates orders. Discount ladder below is deliberately tight, and the two
highest-intent cohorts get **zero** discount.

**Fact 4 — Repeat rate is 7.6%. That is the biggest untapped pot.**
180 days: 2,812 customers, only 213 returning. Your "Orders – 180 Days" cohort (2,635) is
essentially your whole customer base, and it buys once and disappears. Diwali is the
natural second purchase. This cohort gets **access and status, never markdown** — margin
here is ~100% because there is no ad cost.

**Funnel context (30 days):** 204,285 sessions → 4,940 add-to-cart (2.4%) → 799 reached
checkout. The ATC→checkout leak is where WhatsApp pays for itself.
