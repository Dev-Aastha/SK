# Notice 5 — Payment Gateway Complaint  ⭐ SABSE ZYADA ASAR

**Ye sabse pehle bhejein.** Hosting band karo to wo 24 ghante mein shift kar lenge.
Payment band ho gaya to business hi ruk jaayega — aur naya merchant account lena
mushkil hota hai, kyunki ab unka record kharab hai.

---

## Step 1 — Gateway identify karein (5 minute)

Unke site par cart mein item daalein aur checkout tak jaayein (payment **mat**
karein). Checkout page par gateway ka naam dikh jaayega:

- **Razorpay** — URL mein `razorpay.com` ya "Powered by Razorpay"
- **Cashfree** — `cashfree.com`
- **PayU** — `payu.in`
- **Paytm** — `paytm.in`
- **Instamojo**, **PhonePe**, **CCAvenue** — apne branding ke saath

Page ka **screenshot** lein — gateway ka naam aur merchant name dono dikhne chahiye.
Ho sake to page source mein `key_id` ya merchant ID bhi dhoond lein, wo complaint
mein bahut helpful hota hai.

- **Trademark:** DEV AASTHA, Indian Registration No. [TM NUMBER], Class [CLASS],
  registered [DATE] (certificate attached)

### The merchant

- **Website:** https://vashome.shop
- **Payment gateway observed at checkout:** [GATEWAY NAME] (screenshot attached)
- **Merchant ID / key observed:** [IF FOUND]
- **Observed on:** [DATE]

### The issue

vashome.shop is a wholesale clone of our website. It reproduces our site design and
structure, our original product photography, and our original product descriptions,
and it displays our registered trademark DEV AASTHA without any authorisation from
us. Full URL-by-URL evidence with archived copies is attached.

The merchant is collecting payments from the public for goods presented under our
brand and our product imagery. Consumers transacting on that site have reason to
believe they are buying from Dev Aastha. They are not. We have no relationship with
this merchant and cannot fulfil, service, support or refund any order placed there.

### Why this is your risk

- **Chargeback exposure.** Customers who believe they bought from Dev Aastha and
  receive different goods — or nothing — will dispute those transactions.
- **Onboarding integrity.** The merchant has, in substance, presented another
  entity's brand and catalogue as its own storefront. This bears on the accuracy of
  the information given at onboarding and on ongoing KYC.
- **Regulatory exposure.** Facilitating payments for a storefront trading on
  another party's registered trademark raises compliance concerns for the acquiring
  institution.

### Requested action

1. Investigate the merchant account associated with vashome.shop.
2. Suspend payment processing for that merchant pending investigation.
3. Confirm in writing what action has been taken, with a reference number.

We are separately notifying the hosting provider, the domain registrar and Google.
We reserve all legal remedies, including proceedings for trademark infringement and
passing off.

Sincerely,

[YOUR FULL NAME]
[YOUR TITLE], Dev Aastha
[DATE]
