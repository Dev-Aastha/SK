## The abuse

and structure, our original product photography, and our original product
descriptions, and it displays our registered Indian trademark "DEV AASTHA"
(Registration No. [TM NUMBER], Class [CLASS], registered [DATE]) without any
authorisation from us.

The site is configured to appear to consumers as our business or as an authorised
outlet of it. It accepts orders and payments from the public on that basis. This
is not an isolated copied image — it is a reproduction of an entire commercial
storefront, operated for financial gain.

Full URL-by-URL evidence, with archived copies, is attached.

## Why this concerns you as registrar

The domain is being used in a manner that infringes registered intellectual
property rights and that is likely to cause consumer harm through confusion as to
the origin of goods. We ask you to act under your Acceptable Use / Anti-Abuse
Policy, and, where applicable, under ICANN's requirements on registrar handling of
abuse reports.

### Note on UDRP

Agar registrar action na le, tab UDRP soch sakte hain — **lekin dhyan rahe**,
UDRP ka pehla element hai "domain is identical or confusingly similar to the
complainant's trademark". `vashome` mein "Dev Aastha" nahi hai, to ye element
kamzor padta hai. Paise kharch karne se pehle IP attorney se confirm karein.
Content ke andar trademark ka use alag aur strong ground hai — wo hosting,
payment aur Google wale channels se cover ho raha hai.
