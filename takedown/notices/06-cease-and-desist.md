# Notice 6 — Cease and Desist

⚠️ **Ye apne lawyer ke letterhead par bhejein.** Advocate ke letterhead par aane
wale notice ka asar khud bheje gaye email se kai guna zyada hota hai, aur agar baad
mein court jaana pada to ye record ka hissa banta hai.

**Kab bhejein:** Notices 1–5 file karne ke **baad**. Pehle nahi — warna wo evidence
delete kar denge.

**Kaise bhejein:** Email + registered post/courier, dono. Delivery proof rakhein.

**Kise bhejein:** whois se registrant email/address, site ke contact page se, aur
"Contact Us" / "About" par jo bhi entity naam mile.

---

**WITHOUT PREJUDICE — SAVE AS TO COSTS**

**BY EMAIL AND REGISTERED POST**

To,
[REGISTRANT NAME / ENTITY NAME]
[ADDRESS FROM WHOIS OR SITE]

Date: [DATE]

**Subject: Cease and Desist — Infringement of Registered Trademark and Copyright,
and Passing Off — www.vashome.shop**

Dear Sir/Madam,

We act for **Dev Aastha** ("our client"), proprietor of the registered trademark
**DEV AASTHA** and owner of the copyright in the original photographic and literary
works published at https://www.devaastha.com.

## 1. Our client's rights

1.1 Our client is the registered proprietor of the trademark **DEV AASTHA**,
Registration No. **[TM NUMBER]** in Class **[CLASS]**, registered on **[DATE]**
under the Trade Marks Act, 1999. The registration is valid and subsisting.

1.2 Our client is the owner of the copyright, under the Copyright Act, 1957, in the
original product photographs and original product descriptions published on its
website from **[EARLIEST PUBLICATION DATE]** onwards.

1.3 Our client has traded under the DEV AASTHA name since **[YEAR]** and has
acquired substantial goodwill and reputation in that name in India.

## 2. Your infringing conduct

2.1 You are operating the website **www.vashome.shop**, which we have determined to
be a wholesale reproduction of our client's website. It copies the site's layout
and structure, reproduces our client's original product photographs and product
descriptions verbatim, and displays our client's registered trademark.

2.2 Particulars of the infringement, identifying each infringing page against the
original work it reproduces, are set out in the Schedule annexed hereto, together
with archived copies and screenshots.

2.3 You have at no time sought or obtained any licence, consent or authorisation
from our client.

## 3. The causes of action

Your conduct constitutes:

(a) **Infringement of a registered trademark** under Section 29 of the Trade Marks
Act, 1999, including use of a mark identical to our client's registered mark in
relation to identical goods;

(b) **Infringement of copyright** under Section 51 of the Copyright Act, 1957, by
reproduction and publication of our client's original artistic and literary works;

(c) **Passing off**, by representing your goods and business as those of our
client, or as connected with our client in the course of trade;

(d) **Unfair competition**, and deception of consumers who transact with you in the
belief that they are dealing with our client.

## 4. Demands

You are required, **within seven (7) days** of the date of this notice, to:

(a) **Cease and desist** all use of the trademark DEV AASTHA and all use of our
client's copyrighted photographs and product descriptions;

(b) **Take down** www.vashome.shop, or remove all infringing material from it;

(c) **Remove** all infringing material from every social media account, marketplace
listing, advertisement and other channel under your control;

(d) **Confirm in writing** that you have complied, and undertake not to repeat the
infringing conduct;

(e) **Disclose** the total revenue earned from the infringing website, together
with the number of orders taken, and details of your suppliers and of any other
domains or storefronts you operate reproducing our client's material;

(f) **Transfer** the domain www.vashome.shop to our client, or, at our client's
election, permanently cease its use.

## 5. If you do not comply

Should you fail to comply within the stated period, our client will initiate
proceedings without further notice, seeking:

- a permanent injunction;
- damages, or at our client's election an account of profits;
- delivery up and destruction of infringing material;
- costs of the proceedings.

Our client will also pursue criminal remedies available under Section 103 and
Section 104 of the Trade Marks Act, 1999, and Section 63 of the Copyright Act, 1957.

## 6. Preservation of evidence

You are put on notice to **preserve all documents and electronic records** relating
to www.vashome.shop, including website files, server logs, order records, payment
records, supplier correspondence and communications. Destruction or alteration of
such records will be brought to the court's attention.

## 7. Reservation of rights

Nothing in this notice constitutes a waiver of any of our client's rights, all of
which are expressly reserved.
